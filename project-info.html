PAWLY Roadmap

发展路线图 / Development Roadmap

Q1
PWA + dApp 上线 Live
主站 PWA（www.pawlypets.online）与专业版 dApp 已上线。支持邮箱登录、钱包连接、成员数据与官网 APK 分发。正式域名与基础品牌入口完成。
Main PWA (www.pawlypets.online) and professional dApp are live. Email login, wallet connect, member data and official APK distribution. Production domain and core product entry are ready.
Q2
Payment · Transfer · Swap · Charity Live
链上支付、转账、兑换与慈善捐赠已打通。兼容 Phantom、Solflare、Trust、Coinbase、Bitget、Jupiter 等多钱包；支持扫码收款/付款。成功交易写入统计，供后期奖励依据（无需仅依赖 PWA 注册）。
On-chain payment, transfer, swap and charity are live across major wallets (Phantom, Solflare, Trust, Coinbase, Bitget, Jupiter, etc.), including QR pay. Successful txs are recorded for future rewards — not limited to PWA sign-ups.
Q3
PAWLY Token + Liquidity Pool + LP Lock
发布官方 Token CA；建立主要流动性池并执行 LP 锁仓。按 Unlock Schedule：2 年、4 期、每期 6 个月、每期释放总供应 15%。正式 CA 仅通过官网与官方 X 公布。用户可在市场上买入 PAWLY 用于支付、兑换与捐赠。
Publish official Token CA; launch main LP with lock. Unlock: 2 years, 4 stages, 6 months each, 15% of supply per stage. CA only via official site and X. Users can buy PAWLY for payment, swap and charity.
Q4
PAWLY Token Staking Pool
上线 PAWLY 本币质押池。质押收益来自 30% 奖励分配；奖励代币按 Stage 4 之后的 3 年、6 期计划释放（每期 5% 总供应）。早期用户与链上活跃地址的空投/资格规则将另行公告。
Launch native PAWLY staking. Rewards from the 30% allocation; emissions follow the 3-year, 6-stage plan after Stage 4 (5% of supply each stage). Airdrop/eligibility rules for early and on-chain users will be announced separately.
Q5
USDT / USDC / SOL Staking Pools
在 Stage 4 及 PAWLY 质押稳定运行之后，逐步开放 USDT、USDC、SOL 等质押池，丰富收益选择，并与宠物支付与慈善生态协同。具体 APY、锁仓条件以当时公告与合约为准。
After Stage 4 and stable PAWLY staking, open USDT, USDC and SOL staking pools to broaden yield options alongside pet payment and charity. APY and lock terms follow announcements and contracts at launch.
Q1–Q2 已完成；Q3–Q5 时间以产品与合约就绪为准，官方会提前在网站与 X 通知。
Q1–Q2 are complete. Q3–Q5 timing depends on product and contract readiness; notice will be given on the site and X.
